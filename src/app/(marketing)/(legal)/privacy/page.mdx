# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

This policy explains how Turistar processes personal data when you browse the website, create an account, plan a trip, or share an itinerary.

## 1. Who operates Turistar

Turistar is an independent, open-source project operated by André Marinho. Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to [hey@andremarinho.me](mailto:hey@andremarinho.me).

## 2. Data we process

### 2.1. Website access

Our hosting and security providers may process request data such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, timestamps, and security signals. This data is used to deliver the site, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse.

### 2.2. Account and profile

When you create an account, we process your email address, authentication identifiers, session data, profile slug, and any profile information you choose to provide.

### 2.3. Trips and collaboration

We process the information you add to Turistar, including destinations, dates, activities, notes, addresses, map coordinates, expenses, cover images, and member access. We also process the events needed to save and synchronize changes within a shared plan.

### 2.4. Location search

When you search for a destination, place, or address, the search text and relevant location context are sent through Turistar's server to Geoapify so that matching places can be returned.

### 2.5. Public itineraries

A plan remains private unless an authorized user publishes it. Published itineraries can be viewed by anyone and may appear in Turistar's public trip discovery. Do not publish personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

## 3. How we use data

We process data to:

- Create and secure accounts and sessions.
- Save, display, and synchronize trip plans.
- Enable collaboration and public sharing chosen by an authorized user.
- Return location and map results.
- Maintain the service, diagnose failures, and prevent misuse.
- Respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

Turistar does not sell personal data.

## 4. Service providers and disclosure

Turistar relies on service providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, realtime synchronization, maps, and location search. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or others.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage

Turistar uses essential cookies or similar browser storage for authentication, security, and app preferences. The application does not include advertising trackers or a marketing analytics SDK as of the date above.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Account and trip data is retained while it is needed to provide the service. Deleted plans may remain for a limited period in backups or provider logs before being removed under the provider's retention process. Some records may be retained when required for security or legal obligations.

You can delete individual plans in the app. To request access, correction, or deletion of account-level data, email [hey@andremarinho.me](mailto:hey@andremarinho.me).

## 7. International processing

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we use the protections required by data protection law for those transfers.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to confirm processing, access personal data, correct incomplete or inaccurate data, request deletion or restriction, obtain information about sharing, or object to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## 9. Security

We use access controls and service-level safeguards intended to protect data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid adding sensitive or confidential information to a trip.

## 10. Children

Turistar is not directed to children. Do not create an account if you cannot legally agree to this policy in your jurisdiction without a parent or guardian.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: [hey@andremarinho.me](mailto:hey@andremarinho.me).
